HomeStash 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 20 日 · 適用於 Android 與 iOS；可用功能依安裝版本而定。

HomeStash 不登入也能用，未使用雲端同步時，庫存資料與照片保留在手機上。你選擇登入並使用雲端同步（Pro 或既有永久同步權益）時，才會將這些內容上傳，讓同一帳號在不同裝置看到相同資料。登入、購買及還原購買也需要與相關服務連線。

不投放廣告、不做使用行為分析、不賣資料、不做廣告追蹤。你可以在 App 內或依刪除帳號說明提出刪除要求。

先說最重要的改變

先前的 HomeStash 沒有網路權限，資料從來沒有離開過你的手機，當時的政策寫的是「不蒐集、不傳輸、不分享任何個人資料」。

當時我們也寫過，未來的雲端功能會「預設關閉、由你主動開啟」。2026 年 9 月曾有幾個版本必須登入才能使用；目前已恢復不登入也能管理庫存的方式。雲端同步需登入並具備同步權益，免費離線使用不需登入。

先前就在使用雲端同步的帳號，同步永久免費。從舊版更新上來時，你手機裡原有的資料不會消失，也不需要重新建立。

為什麼蒐集

用於登入、連結登入方式、同步及還原你自己的資料、確認 Pro 購買歸屬與有效期間，以及必要的安全維運。登入方式只有在伺服器確認屬於同一個 HomeStash 帳號後才會連結；不會因電子郵件文字相同就搬移既有庫存或購買權益。

支援購買轉移的版本會在你明確確認並授權原購買帳號後，驗證 Apple 交易並變更這筆購買的 HomeStash 帳號歸屬。轉移不合併或搬移庫存與照片，也不再次收費。原帳號授權憑證僅用於驗證，不寫入購買紀錄。伺服器保存必要的交易鏈、帳號識別碼雜湊、目前與前一歸屬及轉移時間／次數，用於還原、續訂、退款與防止重複使用。

不用於廣告、不用於分析、不用於建立使用者輪廓，也不會用來訓練任何模型。

傳給誰

我們使用 Supabase 提供資料庫、照片儲存及登入驗證。Supabase 依其服務與資料保護條款處理相關資料，包含提供服務所需的安全及維運紀錄。

Google 與 Apple 依你選擇的登入方式處理驗證；Google Play 與 Apple App Store 依使用平台處理付款。我們的伺服器向對應商店查詢及驗證購買。登入與商店服務也適用各自的隱私權政策。

我們不賣資料，也不與資料仲介或廣告商交換資料。上述服務可能在你居住地以外處理資料。

存多久

只要你的帳號存在，庫存資料就會保存。刪除帳號時，伺服器上的帳號、庫存與照片資料會一併刪除。曾轉移的 Apple 購買會清除對照紀錄中的帳號識別碼，保留交易鏈、不可直接識別帳號的雜湊與轉移紀錄，避免舊交易被重新授權到歷史帳號；已轉至其他現存帳號的購買紀錄仍用於提供該帳號的權益。

手機上那一份資料留在你的裝置裡，直到你刪除帳號、清除 app 儲存空間，或解除安裝。

刪除作業清除運作中的帳號、庫存及照片資料；服務供應商的備份及必要安全紀錄可能依其保留機制稍後清除。你自行匯出的備份、CSV 或相簿原檔，需由你自行刪除。

怎麼刪除

在 App 裡打開設定 › 帳號與同步 › 刪除帳號。確認後會刪除目前帳號的雲端資料，並清除這台手機上屬於該帳號的資料；其他帳號的資料不受影響。

刪除 HomeStash 帳號或移除 App 不會自動取消商店訂閱。如需停止續訂，請到原購買商店的訂閱管理頁取消。Google／Apple 登入授權也可在各提供者的帳號設定中管理。

如果你已經無法開啟 app（換機、解除安裝、裝置遺失），請依帳號刪除說明來信申請。

刪除單一物品或收納位置也是真的刪除。它會從你所有裝置上消失，名稱、備註、購買資訊與照片檔案都會從伺服器清掉。伺服器上只留下一筆「這個編號已刪除」的紀錄，因為那是你其他裝置得知它被刪掉的唯一方式 —— 那筆紀錄不包含你輸入過的任何內容。

刪除是永久的，沒有寬限期，也沒有復原管道。想保留資料，請先在設定裡匯出一份完整備份或 CSV。

我們怎麼保護它

  • 所有連線一律使用 HTTPS；app 已停用明文連線。
  • 資料庫每一張表都啟用了列級安全性，每一列都綁定所屬帳號，別的帳號查不到也改不到。
  • 照片存放在非公開的儲存空間，只有你的帳號能存取自己的資料夾。
  • 登入憑證透過平台安全儲存機制保存；iOS 使用 Keychain，Android 使用對應的安全儲存實作。
  • Android 的自動備份與裝置間轉移已關閉 —— 這個資料庫記錄的是你有什麼、放在哪裡。

沒有任何系統能保證絕對安全，但以上是我們實際採取的措施，不是原則性的說法。

HomeStash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Covers Android and iOS; available features depend on the installed version.

HomeStash works without an account. Inventory and photos stay on your device when you do not use cloud sync. Signing in and using cloud sync with Pro or an existing permanent sync entitlement uploads that content so the same account can access it across devices. Sign-in, purchases and restoration also contact the relevant services.

No advertising, behavioural analytics, data sales or advertising tracking. You can request account deletion in the app or follow our account deletion instructions.

The important change, first

The previous HomeStash had no internet permission. Nothing ever left your phone, and the policy at the time said we collected, transmitted and shared nothing.

We previously said cloud features would be optional. Some versions in September 2026 required sign-in; account-free inventory management has since been restored. Cloud sync requires sign-in and a sync entitlement. Free offline use does not require an account.

Accounts that were already using cloud sync keep it free, permanently. If you are updating from an earlier version, the data on your phone is kept; nothing has to be entered again.

The app does not access device location, contacts, calls, messages or health data, and contains no advertising identifiers, advertising SDKs or behavioural analytics SDKs. Place names or notes you type are inventory content, not device location readings.

Sign-in, sync and purchase verification may generate necessary connection, security and error records at service providers, such as request time, network address and response status. These support operations and troubleshooting, not advertising tracking. HomeStash purchase diagnostics do not log full transaction contents, sign-in tokens or private keys.

App-processed photos have GPS and other EXIF metadata removed. App-managed copies are stored privately; original gallery photos and copies you export are not deleted by that processing.

Why we collect it

For sign-in, linking sign-in methods, syncing and restoring your own data, checking Pro ownership and validity, and necessary security operations. A sign-in method is linked only after the server confirms the same HomeStash account; matching email text does not move existing inventory or purchases between accounts.

Versions supporting purchase transfers verify the Apple transaction and change its HomeStash account ownership only after your explicit confirmation and authorization from the original purchase account. Transfers do not merge or move inventory or photos, and do not charge again. Original-account credentials are used for verification and are not stored in purchase records. The server retains the necessary transaction chain, account-identifier hash, current and previous ownership, and transfer time/count for restoration, renewals, refunds and preventing duplicate use.

Not for advertising, not for analytics, not to build a profile of you, and not to train any model.

Who it goes to

We use Supabase for the database, photo storage and authentication. Supabase processes relevant data under its service and data protection terms, including security and operational records needed to provide the service.

Google and Apple authenticate the sign-in method you choose. Google Play and the Apple App Store process payments on the relevant platform; our server queries the corresponding store to verify purchases. These services also have their own privacy policies.

We do not sell data or exchange it with data brokers or advertisers. These services may process data outside your country of residence.

How long we keep it

Inventory is retained while your account exists. Deleting the account removes its active account, inventory and photo records. For previously transferred Apple purchases, account identifiers are cleared from transfer bindings; the transaction chain, a hash that does not directly identify the account, and transfer records are retained to prevent old transactions from granting access to a historical owner again. A purchase transferred to another existing account remains recorded to provide that account's entitlement.

The copy on your phone stays there until you delete your account, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it.

Deletion clears active account, inventory and photo records. Provider backups and necessary security records may be cleared later under their retention processes. You must separately delete exported backups, CSVs and original gallery files you control.

How to delete it

In the app, open Settings › Account & sync › Delete account. After confirmation, the current account's cloud records and its data on this device are deleted. Other accounts' data is not affected.

Deleting a HomeStash account or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel a store subscription. Cancel renewal in the original store's subscription settings. Google and Apple sign-in authorizations can also be managed in the provider's account settings.

If you can no longer open the app — new phone, uninstalled, device lost — follow the account deletion instructions to request it by email.

Deleting a single item or storage location really deletes it. It disappears from every device you use, and its name, notes, purchase details and photo file are cleared from the server. What remains is a record that this id was deleted, because that is how your other devices learn it is gone -- it holds none of what you typed.

Deletion is permanent. There is no grace period and no way to undo it. Export a full backup or a CSV first if you want to keep anything.

How we protect it

  • Every connection uses HTTPS; cleartext traffic is disabled in the app.
  • Every database table has row level security enabled, and every row is bound to the account that owns it, so another account can neither read it nor change it.
  • Photos are kept in a private storage bucket where an account can only reach its own folder.
  • Sign-in credentials use platform-secure storage: Keychain on iOS and the corresponding secure storage implementation on Android.
  • Android auto-backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off — this database records what you own and where you keep it.

No system can promise to be perfectly secure, but the above is what is actually in place rather than a statement of intent.

Permissions

  • Internet: for sign-in, cloud sync, purchases, restoration and verification when those connected features are used.
  • Camera: for storage QR scanning and taking photos, with authorization requested as required by the platform and feature. Live scan footage is not uploaded; photos you choose to save follow your sync settings.
  • Photo selection: the system picker provides the photos you select; the app does not scan or upload your entire gallery.
  • Notifications: for warranty and expiry reminders. You are only asked when you turn them on in Settings, and the app works without them.

No location or contacts permission is requested. Expiry reminders are scheduled on your device without requiring inventory uploads; reminder settings control whether and how early they are sent.
