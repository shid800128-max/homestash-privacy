HomeStash 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 19 日

這一版的 HomeStash 需要登入，而且會把你的庫存資料與照片上傳到我們的伺服器。目的只有一個：讓同一個帳號在你的不同裝置之間看到同一份資料。

不投放廣告、不做分析、不賣資料、沒有任何第三方追蹤程式。你可以隨時刪除帳號，一次刪乾淨。

先說最重要的改變

先前的 HomeStash 沒有網路權限，資料從來沒有離開過你的手機，當時的政策寫的是「不蒐集、不傳輸、不分享任何個人資料」。

當時我們也寫過，未來的雲端功能會「預設關閉、由你主動開啟」。這一點我們沒有做到：這一版是登入之後才能使用，沒有純離線模式可選。與其把它藏在條文裡，不如直接寫在最前面 —— 如果這不是你要的，請不要更新；已經更新的話，可以在設定裡刪除帳號，並改用匯出的備份。

從舊版更新上來時，你手機裡原有的資料會在第一次登入時歸到該帳號底下，不會消失，也不需要重新建立。

我們蒐集什麼

照片在離開手機之前，拍攝時記錄的 GPS 座標等中繼資料會被清除。照片存放在 app 的私有目錄，不會出現在你手機的相簿裡。

為什麼蒐集

只為了讓功能運作：把你的資料同步到你自己的其他裝置，以及讓你換手機之後能把東西拿回來。電子郵件地址由 Google 在你登入時提供，用來辨識你的帳號。

不用於廣告、不用於分析、不用於建立使用者輪廓，也不會用來訓練任何模型。

傳給誰

資料存放在 Supabase（資料庫、檔案儲存與登入驗證），伺服器位於日本東京。Supabase 是代我們處理資料的服務供應商，依我們的指示保管資料，不會為自己的目的使用它。

除此之外，我們不會把你的資料提供給任何第三方。我們不賣資料，也不與資料仲介或廣告商交換資料。

存多久

只要你的帳號存在，資料就會一直保存 —— 這正是它的用途。你刪除帳號時，伺服器上的照片與所有資料列會一併刪除。

手機上那一份資料留在你的裝置裡，直到你刪除帳號、清除 app 儲存空間，或解除安裝。

怎麼刪除

在 app 裡打開設定 › 帳號 › 刪除帳號。畫面會先告訴你將被刪除的數量，你確認之後才會執行：先刪伺服器上的照片，再刪帳號本身，最後清除這台手機上的資料。

如果你已經無法開啟 app（換機、解除安裝、裝置遺失），請依帳號刪除說明來信申請。

刪除單一物品或收納位置也是真的刪除。它會從你所有裝置上消失，名稱、備註、購買資訊與照片檔案都會從伺服器清掉。伺服器上只留下一筆「這個編號已刪除」的紀錄，因為那是你其他裝置得知它被刪掉的唯一方式 —— 那筆紀錄不包含你輸入過的任何內容。

刪除是永久的，沒有寬限期，也沒有復原管道。想保留資料，請先在設定裡匯出一份完整備份或 CSV。

我們怎麼保護它

  • 所有連線一律使用 HTTPS；app 已停用明文連線。
  • 資料庫每一張表都啟用了列級安全性，每一列都綁定所屬帳號，別的帳號查不到也改不到。
  • 照片存放在非公開的儲存空間，只有你的帳號能存取自己的資料夾。
  • 登入憑證存放在 Android 的加密儲存區，不是明文設定檔。
  • Android 的自動備份與裝置間轉移已關閉 —— 這個資料庫記錄的是你有什麼、放在哪裡。

沒有任何系統能保證絕對安全，但以上是我們實際採取的措施，不是原則性的說法。

權限

  • 網路：用於同步。這是唯一會把資料送出這台手機的地方。
  • 通知：用於保固與有效期限提醒。你在設定裡打開時才會詢問，不開也能正常使用。

不要求相機權限（拍照走系統相機）、不要求相簿權限（走系統相片選擇器，只看得到你選的那幾張）、不要求位置權限、不要求通訊錄權限。

兒童

本 app 不以兒童為對象，也不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

政策更新時會修改本頁最上方的日期。涉及蒐集範圍的變更，會在 app 內另行說明。

HomeStash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This version of HomeStash requires an account, and it uploads your inventory and photos to our server. There is one reason for that: so the same account sees the same data on every device you use.

No advertising, no analytics, no data sales, no third-party tracking of any kind. You can delete your account at any time, and it deletes everything.

The important change, first

The previous HomeStash had no internet permission. Nothing ever left your phone, and the policy at the time said we collected, transmitted and shared nothing.

We also said that cloud features, when they arrived, would be off by default and switched on by you. We did not keep that promise: this version requires you to sign in before you can use it, and there is no offline-only mode to choose. That belongs at the top of this page rather than buried in it. If it is not what you want, do not update; if you already have, you can delete your account in Settings and keep using an exported backup.

If you are updating from the offline version, the data already on your phone is attached to your account the first time you sign in. Nothing is lost and nothing has to be entered again.

We do not collect: your location, contacts, calls or messages, health data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, crash logs, or any behavioural analytics. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

Metadata such as GPS coordinates recorded by the camera is stripped from photos before they leave the phone. Photos live in the app's private storage and never appear in your phone's gallery.

Why we collect it

Only to make the feature work: to sync your data to your own other devices, and to give it back to you on a new phone. Your email address comes from Google when you sign in, and identifies your account.

Not for advertising, not for analytics, not to build a profile of you, and not to train any model.

Who it goes to

Your data is stored with Supabase (database, file storage and authentication), on servers in Tokyo, Japan. Supabase processes it on our behalf and under our instructions, and does not use it for its own purposes.

Beyond that, we do not give your data to anyone. We do not sell it and we do not trade it with data brokers or advertisers.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists — that is what it is for. When you delete your account, the photos and every row belonging to it are deleted with it.

The copy on your phone stays there until you delete your account, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it.

How to delete it

In the app, open Settings › Account › Delete account. You are shown how much will be deleted before anything happens: photos on the server go first, then the account itself, then the data on this phone.

If you can no longer open the app — new phone, uninstalled, device lost — follow the account deletion instructions to request it by email.

Deleting a single item or storage location really deletes it. It disappears from every device you use, and its name, notes, purchase details and photo file are cleared from the server. What remains is a record that this id was deleted, because that is how your other devices learn it is gone -- it holds none of what you typed.

Deletion is permanent. There is no grace period and no way to undo it. Export a full backup or a CSV first if you want to keep anything.

How we protect it

  • Every connection uses HTTPS; cleartext traffic is disabled in the app.
  • Every database table has row level security enabled, and every row is bound to the account that owns it, so another account can neither read it nor change it.
  • Photos are kept in a private storage bucket where an account can only reach its own folder.
  • Sign-in credentials are held in Android's encrypted storage, not in a plaintext preferences file.
  • Android auto-backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off — this database records what you own and where you keep it.

No system can promise to be perfectly secure, but the above is what is actually in place rather than a statement of intent.

Permissions

  • Internet: for sync. It is the only thing that sends anything off this phone.
  • Notifications: for warranty and expiry reminders. You are only asked when you turn them on in Settings, and the app works without them.

No camera permission (the system camera is used), no gallery permission (the system photo picker is used, which shows us only what you picked), no location permission, no contacts permission.

Children

This app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

The date at the top of this page changes when the policy does. Changes to what we collect will also be explained inside the app.
