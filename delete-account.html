刪除 HomeStash 帳號

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 15 日　·　回到隱私權政策

你可以隨時刪除 HomeStash 帳號與其中的所有資料。刪除是永久的，沒有寬限期，也沒有復原管道 —— 想留下資料請先在 app 內匯出一份完整備份。

如果你從未登入過

純離線使用時不存在任何帳號，你的資料也從未離開過這支手機。此時刪除全部資料的方式是 Android 的「設定 › 應用程式 › HomeStash › 儲存空間 › 清除儲存空間」，或直接解除安裝本 app，無須向我們提出任何申請。

判斷方式很簡單：打開 HomeStash 的「設定」，如果沒有看到「帳號」這一區，就表示你沒有帳號，這一頁其餘內容不適用於你。

在 App 內刪除（最快）

開啟 HomeStash，依序點選設定 › 帳號 › 刪除帳號。

畫面會先告訴你即將被刪除的地點與物品數量，你確認之後才會執行。完成後 app 會回到登入畫面。

用電子郵件申請

如果你已經無法開啟 app —— 換了手機、解除安裝、或裝置遺失 —— 請從你註冊時使用的電子郵件地址寄信至 boen520520@gmail.com，主旨寫「刪除帳號」即可。

我們會以寄件地址核對身分，在收到後 30 天內完成刪除，並回信通知。

會刪除哪些資料

帳號刪除後，以下資料會被永久刪除：

  • 你的帳號與登入資訊（電子郵件地址）
  • 雲端上全部的地點、收納位置、物品，以及其備註、分類、數量與購買資訊
  • 雲端上全部的照片，包含發票與收據照片
  • 物品與照片之間的關聯資料

從 app 內刪除時，該台裝置上的本機資料也會一併刪除。如果你曾在其他裝置上登入同一個帳號，請在那些裝置上再開啟一次 app：帳號已不存在，app 會回到登入畫面。

保留期限

帳號刪除為立即執行，我們不保留任何寬限期，也沒有復原管道。如果你想留下這些資料，請在刪除前先使用「設定 › 匯出完整備份」匯出一份備份檔。

系統層級的災難備援快照最多在 30 天後自動覆寫。這些快照不會被存取，也不會用於任何用途。

聯絡方式

對刪除流程有任何疑問，請寄信至 boen520520@gmail.com。

Delete your HomeStash account

Last updated: 15 September 2026  ·  Back to the privacy policy

You can delete your HomeStash account and everything in it at any time. Deletion is permanent: there is no grace period and no way to undo it — export a full backup from inside the app first if you want to keep anything.

If you never signed in

Used offline, there is no account and your data has never left your phone. To delete all of it, use Android's Settings › Apps › HomeStash › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. There is no request to make of us.

To check: open HomeStash and go to Settings. If there is no "Account" section, you do not have an account and the rest of this page does not apply to you.

In the app (fastest)

Open HomeStash and go to Settings › Account › Delete account.

You will be told how many places and items are about to be deleted, and nothing happens until you confirm. Afterwards the app returns to the sign-in screen.

By email

If you can no longer open the app — new phone, uninstalled, or device lost — email us from the address you signed up with: boen520520@gmail.com, with "Delete my account" as the subject.

We verify identity against that address, complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving the request, and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and sign-in details (your email address)
  • Every place, storage spot and item stored in the cloud, with their notes, categories, quantities and purchase details
  • Every photo stored in the cloud, including receipts
  • The links between items and photos

Deleting from inside the app also deletes the local copy on that device. If you signed in to the same account on other devices, open the app once on each: the account no longer exists, and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

Retention

Deletion is immediate. We keep no grace-period copy and there is no recovery path. If you want to keep your data, export a backup from Settings › Export full backup before you delete.

Infrastructure-level disaster-recovery snapshots are overwritten automatically within 30 days. They are not accessed and are not used for any purpose.
