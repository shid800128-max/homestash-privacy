常見問題

一定要登入嗎？

免費離線功能不需要登入。物品資料可以留在裝置中管理；雲端同步需要登入並具備同步權益。

搜尋到物品後，怎麼知道放在哪裡？

搜尋結果會顯示物品的完整收納路徑。開啟物品可查看所屬地點與收納位置；也可以從「地點」逐層進入櫃子、抽屜或收納箱。

換裝置後看不到雲端資料？

先確認兩台裝置登入的是同一個 HomeStash 帳號，並確認網路連線與同步權益。在 App 的「設定 › 帳號與同步」查看同步狀態；若有錯誤，請將錯誤文字寄給我們。

已購買 Pro，但權益沒有出現？

請先確認登入原先購買時使用的 HomeStash 帳號，以及相同的商店帳號，再於 Pro 頁面按「還原購買」。權益需要完成購買驗證才會更新；還原購買不會把另一個 HomeStash 帳號的購買轉移到目前帳號。

如何取消月訂閱？

App Store 訂閱請到 Apple 帳號的「訂閱」管理；Google Play 訂閱請到 Google Play 的訂閱管理。刪除 App 或 HomeStash 帳號不會取消訂閱。一次性買斷不會自動續訂。

沒有收到到期提醒？

請確認 HomeStash 的提醒設定已開啟、物品已設定有效期限或保固日期，並允許系統通知。提醒依你設定的提前天數，在裝置當地上午 9 點排程；已經過去的提醒時間不會補發。

如何刪除帳號或提出資料刪除申請？

在 App 開啟「設定 › 帳號與同步 › 刪除帳號」，或查看帳號與資料刪除說明。資料處理方式請見隱私權政策。

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Free offline inventory management does not require sign-in. Cloud sync requires sign-in and a sync entitlement.

How do I find a stored item?

Search results show the item's full storage path. Open the item to see its place and storage location, or browse through your cabinets, drawers and boxes.

My cloud data is missing on another device.

Check that both devices use the same HomeStash account, have a network connection and a sync entitlement. Check sync status in Settings under Account & Sync. Include any error text when contacting support.

My Pro purchase is not showing.

Sign in to the HomeStash account used for the original purchase and the same store account, then choose Restore Purchases on the Pro page. Entitlements update after verification. Restore does not transfer another HomeStash account's purchase to your current account.

How do I cancel a monthly subscription?

Manage App Store subscriptions in your Apple account's subscription settings, or Google Play subscriptions in Google Play. Deleting the app or your HomeStash account does not cancel a subscription. A lifetime purchase does not renew automatically.

Why did I not receive an expiry reminder?

Enable reminders in HomeStash, set an expiry or warranty date for the item, and allow system notifications. Reminders are scheduled for 9 a.m. in your device's local time, according to the advance notice you choose. Past reminder times are not replayed.

How do I delete my account or request data deletion?

In the app, open Settings › Account & Sync › Delete Account, or read the account and data deletion instructions. See the privacy policy for information about data handling.
